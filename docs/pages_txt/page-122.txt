Figure II.1(e) shows a radiograph of human feet. In this image, we can
recognize a faint outline of the soft tissue—looking like a conventional footprint.
We see the white outlines of bone and also the washed out appearance of many
overlaying bones in the ankle region including the ﬁbula and tibia. This image
also shows the symbols ‘‘L’’ and ‘‘R’’ indicating which foot is the left and which
is the right, respectively. In this case, left is on our left and right is on our right,
as if we were looking down at our own feet. The markers are made from lead
and are placed by the radiographer near the appropriate anatomy. Without these
markers, it is sometimes difﬁcult or impossible to know left from right—and
this can lead to medical errors.
