5.4.3 Compton Scattering
Earlier, we noted that Compton scattering degrades image quality. The reason
for this is that Compton photons are deﬂected from their ideal straight-line
path, and some are detected in locations away from the correct, straight-line
location. This produces two unwanted results: a decrease in image contrast and
a decrease in SNR.
