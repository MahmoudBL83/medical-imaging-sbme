2.5.5 Rotation
Let us denote by fθ (x, y) the signal
fθ (x, y) = f (x cos θ − y sin θ, x sin θ + y cos θ) . (2.89)
We note that fθ (x, y) is a rotated version of f (x, y), rotated by an angle θ around
the origin (0, 0). If F(u, v) is the Fourier transform of f (x, y), then
F2D(fθ )(u, v) = F(u cos θ − v sin θ, u sin θ + v cos θ) . (2.90)
If f (x, y) is rotated by an angle θ, then its Fourier transform is rotated by angle
θ as well.
