Remember that in Fourier or frequency space we are dealing with the repre-
sentation of an object as sine waves of different frequencies. In medical images,
these are frequencies in space— spatial frequencies. Low spatial frequencies
represent signals that vary slowly across the image; high spatial frequencies rep-
resent signals that vary rapidly across a local region (e.g., at the edges of
structures within an image).
