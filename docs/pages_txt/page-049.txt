2.3.5 Separable Systems
Separable systems form an important class of LSI systems. As with separable
signals, a 2-D LSI system with PSF h(x, y)i sa separable system if there are two
1-D systems with PSFs h1(x) and h2(y), such that
h(x, y) = h1(x)h2(y) . (2.55)
