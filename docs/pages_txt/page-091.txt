Usually, noise in medical imaging systems is the result of a summation of
a large number of independent noise sources. According to the central limit
theorem of probability, a random variable that is the sum of a large number of
independent causes tends to be Gaussian. Therefore, it is often natural to model
noise in medical imaging system by means of a Gaussian random variable.
