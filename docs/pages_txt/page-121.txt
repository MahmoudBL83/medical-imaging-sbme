It is instructive to look at the bones around the knee in Figure II.1(c). Why
do they appear to have bright outlines? We know that bones are ﬁlled with
marrow, which is a soft tissue. Therefore, around the edges, the x-rays have to
travel farther in the bone itself and therefore they experience more attenuation,
yielding a brighter image. If the bones were solid, then they would be brighter
in the middle on a radiograph.
